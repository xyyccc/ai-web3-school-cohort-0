# AI × Web3 School - Personal Learning Journal

> Personal learning journal and proof-of-work for AI × Web3 School Cohort 0.

## About AI × Web3 School

AI × Web3 School 是一个开放的学习社区（learning community），旨在帮助学员掌握 AI 与 Web3 交叉领域的知识与技能。

- **Handbook**: https://aiweb3.school/zh/handbook/
- **WCB Course**: https://web3career.build/zh/programs/AI-Web3-School
- **WCB Learning**: https://web3career.build/zh/programs/AI-Web3-School#tab=learning
